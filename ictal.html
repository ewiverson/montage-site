About

What sets me apart

  • PhD in Electrical and Computer Engineering, with experience designing electrical characterization experiments since graduate school and a decade of R&D in semiconductor device physics
  • Translational research experience: taking research concepts to volume production through experimental design, electrical testing, data analysis, and computational modeling
  • Engineering discipline from industry: clear communication across cross-functional teams, vendors, and customers; data-driven decision making; and modern software development practices
  • A personal stake: my youngest daughter was born with focal cortical dysplasia and has undergone four epilepsy surgeries, including a hemispherotomy. See My Story for more.

Services

  • Time-series analysis for EEG, iEEG/SEEG, and other physiological signals: preprocessing, quality control, feature extraction, and event detection
  • Low-noise measurement system design for bio-signal acquisition: scoping signal-chain architecture, sensor and amplifier selection, and calibration strategy so your system hits its noise floor and dynamic range targets before you commit capital to hardware.
  • Data standardization and sharing prep: BIDS/NWB conversion, metadata, validation, and archive-ready datasets
  • Statistical and simulation modeling: Monte Carlo calibration, power analysis, and model fitting to measured data
  • Research software: packaging lab scripts into tested, documented Python libraries
  • Reproducibility support: version control, automated tests, containerized environments, and methods documentation
  • De-identification and database tooling, built and tested on synthetic data so I never have to handle protected health information

  • Flexible structure: hourly or daily consulting, or fixed-scope projects
